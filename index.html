How to make the BEST chocolate chip cookies EVER.

This includes ones like the very first recorded recipe along with more unorthodox ones, and I've discovered a good method for making consistently good cookies regardless of how you like them.

Steps:

  1. Whisk flour, baking soda, and 3/4 teaspoon of salt in a small bowl.
  2. In a large bowl or in a stand mixer, beat the butter until creamy. Add both sugars and mix until combined and fluffy. Add the egg, vanilla extract, and two tablespoons of water, and beat on low. Then beat in the flour mixture on low and add chocolate to the formed dough.
  3. Chill the dough for several hours! This step is integral to producing the best cookies. Heat the oven to 350 degrees, form the dough into 2 tablespoon balls and place them four at a time on bakign sheets lined with aluminum foil. Transfer trays to a freezer for 15 minutes.
  4. Transfer chilled baking sheets to the oven and bake for 10 minutes. Then, open the oven, lift the pan and drop it on the rack. This ensures crispy edges while maintaining a soft center, do it a couple times in a row to get the cookies flattened. Repeat this every 2 to 3 minutes, until the cookies have been baking for 16 to 18 minutes. When removing from the oven, the edges should be golden brown and crispy, while the enter should still be slightly uncooked (it'll continue cooking on the cooling pan)
  5. Repeat this until all your dough is baked. Now you have a ton of fantastic chocolate chip cookies to enjoy!

Alterations



The recipe above produces cookies that are flat with crispy edges and a soft middle. While this is what I think is the best kind of chocolate chip cookie, you might be more inclined to make something crispier or cakier. If that's the case, you can alter proportions of each ingredient to make your desired product. Some easy changes are:

  1. If you want a crispier cookie, use less flour and consider melted butter as opposed to softened.
  2. For a cakier cookie, chill the dough for longer and consider browned butter. The recipe will take a lot longer but browning the butter will change the flavor profile almost entirely, and they're so delicious.
  3. If you want to change the flavor profile of your cookie, consider getting creative with extra ingredients. Piloncillo (or Panela) adds a rich caramel flavor. Tahini can add nuttiness to a dough, and though highly unorthodox, there are even recipes that include pickle juice in the dough.
