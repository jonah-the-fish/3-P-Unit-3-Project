How to make the BEST chocolate chip cookies EVER.

Preface:

I realize I am very confident in my claim, but it is because I have tried dozens of chocolate chip cookies recipes. This includes ones like the very first recorded recipe along with more unorthodox ones, and I've discovereda good method for making consistently good cookies regardless of how you like them.
NOTE: This is NOT just a recipe, but rather the methods you can use any time you make chocolate chip cookies. Some really good recipes will be at the bottom.

    6. THE Method:

    Ingredients Quantity Reason
    Flour 2 cups Rising agent,
    also affects texture
    Baking soda 1/2 Teaspoon Helps cookies spread,
    better texture
    Butter (Room temp) 2 Sticks
    (1 cup)    		 Improves flavor and
    texture, as well as
    spreading
    Granulated sugar 1 and 1/2 Cups Adds sweetness!
    Brown sugar
    (can be light or dark,
    dark is better)    		 1/2 Cup Adds sweetness and
    makes the cookies
    richer
    Egg 1 large Helps blend all
    ingredients together
    Pure vanilla extract
    (Not flavoring!)    		 1 and 1/2 teaspoons Enhances flavor
    Bittersweet chocolate 6 ounces (can be chips,
    chunks, or both)    		 Does this one even
    need a reason?

    Steps:
    1. Whisk flour, baking soda, and 3/4 teaspoon of salt in a small bowl.
    2. In a large bowl or in a stand mixer, beat the butter until creamy. Add moth sugars and mix until combined and fluffy. Add the egg, vanilla extract, and two tablespoons of water.
